## About this map

Every highlighted country has at least one active QGIS user group. Select a logo or a country to see the group, its contacts and its website. Use the buttons on the right to switch between the globe and a flat map.

You can also browse the [list of user groups]({{< ref "/community/groups/list" >}}) or read [how a user group works]({{< ref "/community/groups" >}}).
